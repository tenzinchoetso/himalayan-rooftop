Our corner of Delhi

Majnu-ka-Tilla is Delhi's Tibetan colony: prayer flags, momo steam and narrow lanes that open onto the Yamuna. We're right here, a few floors up, with the river in view.

01

The rooftop

Glowing tables under the open sky, with the Yamuna below and the Signature Bridge on the skyline. Best at sunset.

02

The café

Indoors there are sofas, flower arches and good coffee. It's an easy place to stay a while, rain or shine.

03

The kitchen

Himalayan classics like momos, sabhale and tingmo, next to pizzas, pastas and Asian plates. Served all day.

A look around

Red flowers, warm light.

Evenings & celebrations

Bring the whole gang.

Birthdays, Rakhi lunches and catch-ups with friends are what this place is for. We've hosted DJ nights, Sip N' Paint sessions and flair shows too. New events go up on Instagram first.
